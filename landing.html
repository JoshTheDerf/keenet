KeeNet

All builds are unsigned for now, so you may have to click past Gatekeeper (macOS) or SmartScreen (Windows) the first time you run KeeNet.

Encrypted on your device

Your vault is encrypted with AES-256 or ChaCha20, and your master password never leaves your device.

KeePass compatible

Open and edit the same .kdbx files you use in KeePass, KeePassXC or any other compatible client.

Desktop, mobile and web

There are apps for macOS, Windows, Linux and Android. You can also run it in any modern browser and install it as a PWA.

Works offline

Once installed, the web app works without a connection. Your files stay on your devices unless you choose to sync them to a cloud service.

Open source

KeeNet is MIT licensed. You can read the code, send in fixes, or host the web app on your own server.

Biometric unlock

On mobile, you can unlock your vault with your fingerprint or face (if your device supports it).
